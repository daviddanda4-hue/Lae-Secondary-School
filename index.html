About Us

Lae Secondary School is one of the leading institutions in Morobe Province, dedicated to academic excellence and holistic student development.

Vision

Academic Programmes

  • Science Stream (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Arts Stream (History, Geography, Literature)
  • Business Stream (Accounting, Economics, Business Studies)

Extracurricular Activities

  • Sports (Rugby, Soccer, Athletics)
  • Clubs (Debating, Science Club, Cultural Group)
  • Community Service Projects

Admissions

Students are admitted based on academic performance and entrance requirements set by the Department of Education.

School Calendar

Classes run from February to November, with breaks in April and September.

Policies

Lae Secondary School enforces strict discipline, academic honesty, and respect for diversity.
